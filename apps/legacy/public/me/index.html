02 // Philosophy :: Beyond the Mockup

The design shouldn’t disappear in development.

[Pareto Principle: the first 80% gets it working. The final 20% makes it feel right.]

The Pareto Principle is a useful reminder: a working interface can arrive before the experience feels complete. That final 20%—motion, alignment, feedback, and accessibility—often takes the most care.

My role is to help Design and Engineering protect that intent together, with practical prototypes and a shared eye for the details.

The finishing details

  • Motion that explains what changed and where to go next.
  • Responsive layouts and typography that hold together.
  • Keyboard, touch, and reduced-motion experiences considered from the start.
